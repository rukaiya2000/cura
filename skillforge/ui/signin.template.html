Cura Consultation assistant

For clinicians

The consultation writes itself. You decide what leaves the room.

Cura joins your appointments, keeps the note, drafts the patient's letter and books what comes next — through your own calendar and records, never its own.

It knows who it is with

The patient comes from the invite you sent, fixed before the meeting exists. Never guessed from a voice on the call.

Every line traces back

Each sentence in the letter links to the moment it came from, so checking it is a matter of seconds. Anything Cura cannot trace is flagged before you send.

“The morning readings are higher — around 9 or 10 before breakfast.”

Nothing reaches a patient unread

Cura drafts. You edit and approve. Only then does it send — and there is still a window to stop it.

It stays with one patient

Ask it to update a different record mid-consultation and it refuses, even though you have permission to. This consultation is about someone else.
